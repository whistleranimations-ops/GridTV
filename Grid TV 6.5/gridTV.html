Welcome to GridTV Help

Everything you need to know to get up and running. GridTV is a browser-based TV interface for live channels, recordings, and an on-demand library — all from M3U playlists you provide.

Getting started
Dismissing the screensaver
When you first open GridTV, or after three hours of inactivity, a screensaver appears with scenic photos and a clock. The bar along the bottom says "press 'Space' to watch tv."
To dismiss the screensaver and open the Home menu, press the Space bar on your keyboard. No other key will dismiss it.
The Home menu
The Home menu is your hub. Across the top are tabs: Live TV, DVR, On Demand, Packages, Search, and Help.
Managing M3U playlists
Loading a live TV playlist
1
Open Home → Packages.
2
Click the Upload Live TV Playlist card.
3
Select one or more .m3u or .m3u8 files.
Loading another playlist adds to your channels. It does not replace existing ones.
Loading an On-Demand playlist
On-Demand playlists are separate from your live channels.
  • Open Home → Packages.
  • Click the Upload On Demand Playlist card.
  • Select your M3U files — same format as live TV, but entries become titles instead of channels.
Removing a playlist
On the Packages tab, find the Loaded Playlists trays at the bottom. Click the red × next to a playlist to remove it.
What's inside an M3U file
Each entry in an M3U has metadata followed by a stream URL. GridTV reads:
  • tvg-logo — the channel's logo image
  • group-title — the channel's category
  • tvg-chno — an explicit channel number (optional)
  • The URL on the next line — the stream source
Bare URLs (one per line) are also supported.
Watching TV & changing channels
Watching a channel
From Home → Live TV → Channels, click any channel card in the list on the left.
Changing to a channel by number
While on the Channels screen or the Guide, just type a channel number.
  • A black box appears in the bottom-right corner.
  • Press Enter to jump.
  • Or stop typing — after about three seconds GridTV jumps automatically.
Fullscreen
Click anywhere on the video to go fullscreen. Press Escape to come back.
Recording with DVR
Starting a recording
Click the red circle button on a channel card, or click ● REC under the video.
Watching a recording
Open Home → DVR and click a finished recording to play it back.
Video on Demand
Browsing, saving, and watching
Your titles appear as a grid of cards. Click any title to open its detail view.
  • Save — adds the title to your Saved list
  • Watch — opens a fullscreen player
Managing On-Demand playlists
Remove an On-Demand playlist from the Loaded On Demand Playlists tray on the Packages tab.
Searching live channels
Find a channel by name
Open Home → Search and type a channel name. Results filter as you type.
  • Use ↑ / ↓ to highlight
  • Press Enter to watch
  • Or click a result
The on-screen keyboard
Typing without a keyboard
When you click into any text box, an on-screen keyboard pops up.
  • Click keys to type
  • Switch between ABC, 123, and #+=
  • Drag the keyboard by its title bar
  • Close it with the ×
What saves automatically
Your data persists between sessions
GridTV stores everything in your browser:
  • Loaded playlists (live and on-demand)
  • Saved on-demand titles
  • DVR recordings
  • Keyboard position
Clearing browser data will wipe everything
Warning: Clearing browser data for GridTV's origin will lose all playlists, saved titles, and DVR recordings.
What happens if you go offline
Automatic offline detection
GridTV checks your connection every few seconds. If it fails, a black screen appears. When you reconnect, everything comes back automatically.
